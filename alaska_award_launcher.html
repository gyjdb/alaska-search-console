Alaska award search launcher

Partner award · transpacific · pick a direction, month, fare type, click

Quick months:
Calendar view shows the whole month, so month-end and month-start dates are both covered by one search. "Lowest price available" surfaces saver-fare days.
How to open: click a button to jump there. To open in a new tab, hold ⌘ (Mac) or Ctrl (Windows) while clicking, or use middle-click. To open all routes from one origin, click "Copy all" and paste anywhere.
TYO: searches both Tokyo airports (HND + NRT) at once. The separate HND / NRT cards are there if you want to isolate one. If a search engine ever rejects the city code, fall back to the individual airport cards.
"Connection" tag: just a rough hint that there's no single nonstop on that pair — calendar view may show blank. The full search or a phone agent can usually piece together a connecting routing. Actual operating carriers and stopovers vary, so treat the tag as a starting point, not the full picture.
Round-trips: Alaska prices each one-way partner award independently — no round-trip discount. Search and ticket each direction on its own, so flexibility on either end helps.
