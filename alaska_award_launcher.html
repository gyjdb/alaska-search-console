Atmos Rewards award launcher

Alaska / Hawaiian (Atmos Rewards) partner awards · US ↔ China & Europe · pick direction, month, cabin & passengers, then click

Quick months:
Calendar view shows the whole month at once. "home" = go back to China (winter / summer break), "back" = return to the US (winter quarter / fall term).
How to open: click a code to jump there. To open in a new tab, hold ⌘ (Mac) or Ctrl (Windows) while clicking, or middle-click. To grab every route for one destination at once, click "Copy all" and paste anywhere.
Tags = the Atmos partner flying that route. Asia: HKG → Cathay · Tokyo → JAL · TPE → Starlux · ICN → Korean Air. Europe: MAD → Iberia · HEL → Finnair · DUB → Aer Lingus · CDG → American · LHR/FRA → American (or BA / Condor). Whether a date is nonstop or connecting — and the mileage price — is exactly what the calendar shows.
Free stopover trick (one per one-way award): US → HKG (stop over) → PVG, or US → gateway (stop over) → onward Europe city, can be a single award instead of two. For Shenzhen, fly Cathay to HKG, then short hop / ferry / ground transfer across the border.
Europe = great value, but dodge fuel surcharges: Iberia (MAD), Finnair (HEL) and Aer Lingus (DUB) run around 45k business one-way from the East Coast with ~$25 in fees — booking via Atmos avoids the surcharges Avios programs add. West Coast lands in a higher band. American metal (CDG / LHR / FRA) has no surcharges either. Avoid British Airways metal — surcharges can top $500 each way.
One partner per award, no round-trip discount: Most awards use a single partner (Atmos only lets you mix among American, British Airways & Finnair so far). Each direction is priced and ticketed on its own — so it is fine to use different partners on the two one-ways, and flexibility on either end helps.
Asia reality + gotchas: Saver premium space on Cathay is brutal (rarely any); JAL business (~60k one-way) is the realistic premium sweet spot, economy is far easier. No fuel surcharges on Cathay / JAL / Starlux / Korean, but Hainan, BA & Icelandair add them. Atmos blocks many partner awards within ~72h of departure, charges a small per-award partner booking fee, and shows "phantom" space that errors at booking — so try alternate dates if one fails.
